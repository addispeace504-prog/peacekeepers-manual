🏛️1. What You Can Do as Sub City Admin

As Sub City Admin, you are the executive administrator for one sub-city. You oversee day-to-day operations, approve certificates, send SMS, and submit community discussion reports.

🔒
Your scope: one sub-city Your permissions are limited to the sub-city you're assigned to. You cannot view or manage data from other sub-cities.

🔐2. Log In to the System

1
Log In
Required for every session
What it does Opens the system with your Sub City Admin identity so you can access sub-city tools and data.
Before you start
  • Your username (given by the administrator who created your account)
  • Your PIN (6+ digits)
  • A modern browser and internet connection
https://www.aapskeeper.gov.et
  1. 1
    Open your browserLaunch Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  2. 2
    Go to the login pageType www.aapskeeper.gov.et and press Enter.
  3. 3
    Wait for the page to loadYou'll see the Addis Ababa city seal and the login form.
  4. 4
    Click the Username fieldA cursor appears inside the box.
  5. 5
    Type your usernameExactly as given by your administrator.
  6. 6
    Click the PIN field
  7. 7
    Type your PINEach digit shows as a dot • • • • • •
  8. 8
    Click Login➜ Login
  9. 9
    Wait for the dashboardLoads in 2–5 seconds.
✅ What happens next
  • You land on the Dashboard
  • Your location label shows your sub-city name
  • The left menu shows Sub City Admin options
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Typing the wrong PIN digits — dots are your only feedback
  • Adding spaces before or after username
  • Caps Lock on
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Invalid username or PIN" → Check spelling and caps, try again
  • "Account locked" → Wait 15 minutes
  • Page won't load → Refresh (F5), check internet, try another browser
  • Forgot PIN → Use "Forgot PIN" on the login page

🔑5. Change Your PIN

Recommended every 3 months
What it does Updates your login PIN. You must know your current PIN to set a new one.

  1. 1
    Open Update AccountClick it in the left menu.

  • "Previous PIN incorrect" → Try again carefully
  • "PIN too short" → At least 6 digits

🔎7. Search & Filter Peace Keepers

6
Find a Specific Peace Keeper
Search by name, ID, phone, or area
What it does Lets you narrow the officer list to find exactly who you're looking for — using search text, dropdown filters, or both together.
Available search fields
  • Search box — type name, ID, or phone
  • District filter — narrow to one woreda
  • Ketena filter — narrow to one neighborhood
  • Block filter — narrow to one block
  • Status filter — Active / Inactive / On Leave / Suspended / Retired
  • Round filter — deployment round
  • Member Type filter — Peace Keepers, Volunteer, NGO, etc.
  1. 1
    Open "View Peace Keepers"The list loads.
  2. 2
    Type in the search box (optional)Enter part of a name, an ID, or a phone number.
  3. 3
    Click "Search"🔍 Search The list updates.
  4. 4
    Combine with filters (optional)Pick District, Ketena, Status, etc., from the dropdowns.
  5. 5
    Click "Apply Filters"if the page has a separate button.
  6. 6
    Review the filtered listOnly matching officers are shown.
  7. 7
    Click "Reset" or "Clear"to start over with the full list.
✅ What happens next
  • You found the officer(s) you were looking for
  • You can open their profile or print the filtered list
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Typing partial ID — must be exact for ID matches
  • Combining too many filters — result may be empty
  • Forgot to click "Search" after typing

  8. 8
    Type actual attendanceActual Male, Actual Female. Actual Total auto-calculates.

⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Typing 0 for "Blocks with Discussion" if a discussion was held

🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Required field missing" → Check the red-highlighted fields
  • "File too large" → Compress the attachment under 10 MB
  • "Invalid date" → Use dd/mm/yyyy format
