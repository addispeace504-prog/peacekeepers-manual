📍1. What You Can Do as Ketena User

As a Ketena User, you are a data entry officer for your own ketena (neighborhood). You register peace keepers, register crime cases, and record attendance — all limited to your ketena.

🔒
Your scope: one ketena Your permissions are limited to the ketena you're assigned to. You cannot view or manage data from other ketenas, districts, or sub-cities.

Your 13 Permissions

# Permission What it does
1Log InAccess the system
2DashboardKetena overview
3Update AccountEdit your own profile
4Change PINUpdate your login PIN
5View Peace KeepersSee all officers in your ketena
6View Crime CasesSee all cases in your ketena
7Attendance Insertion⭐ Record officer attendance
8Attendance DataView attendance records
9Register Peace Keeper⭐ Add new officers in your ketena
10Register Crime⭐ File new crime cases
11Community Discussion ReportSubmit discussion reports
12Community ReportsView submitted reports
13Developer SupportContact support
14Log OutEnd your session
⚠️
What you cannot do You cannot send SMS, manage users, approve certificates, request identity cards, or configure area settings. Those permissions belong to higher roles.

🔐2. Log In to the System

1
Log In
Required for every session
What it does Opens the system with your Ketena identity so you can access ketena-level tools.
Before you start
  • Your username (given by the administrator who created your account)
  • Your PIN (6+ digits)
  • A modern browser and internet connection
https://www.aapskeeper.gov.et
  1. 1
    Open your browserLaunch Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  2. 2
    Go to the login pageType www.aapskeeper.gov.et and press Enter.
  3. 3
    Wait for the page to loadYou'll see the Addis Ababa city seal and login form.
  4. 4
    Click the Username field
  5. 5
    Type your usernameExactly as given.
  6. 6
    Click the PIN field
  7. 7
    Type your PINEach digit shows as a dot.
  8. 8
    Click Login➜ Login
  9. 9
    Wait for the dashboardLoads in 2–5 seconds.
✅ What happens next
  • You land on the Dashboard
  • Your location label shows your ketena
  • The left menu shows Ketena options
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Invalid username or PIN" → Check spelling and caps
  • "Account locked" → Wait 15 minutes
  • Page won't load → Refresh (F5), try another browser
  • Forgot PIN → Use "Forgot PIN" on the login page

📅12. Attendance Insertion

11
Record Peace Keeper Attendance
Mark who is present, late, or absent
What it does Records attendance for peace keepers in your ketena. You can record individuals, check in by QR, or upload a bulk file.
Three attendance methods
  • Individual — type the officer's name and mark status
  • QR Check-In — scan the officer's ID card
  • Bulk Upload — upload a CSV/Excel file for many officers at once
Left menu → Attendance Insertion

Method A — Individual Check-In

  1. 1
    Click "Attendance Insertion"The form opens.
  2. 2
    Type the officer's name in "attName"Dropdown suggests matches.
  3. 3
    Click the officer from the dropdownTheir ID is auto-filled.
  4. 4
    Set "attDate"Ethiopian calendar dd/mm/yyyy.
  5. 5
    Set "attTime"24-hour format.
  6. 6
    Choose "attStatus"Present / Late / Absent / On Leave.
  7. 7
    Type "attNotes" (optional)Reason for late/absent.
  8. 8
    Click "Save Attendance"✓ Save
  9. 9
    Confirm the success message
  10. 10
    Repeat for the next officerForm clears automatically.

Method B — QR Check-In

  1. 1
    Click "Attendance Insertion"
  2. 2
    Choose "QR Check-In"
  3. 3
    Allow camera accessIf prompted, click "Allow".
  4. 4
    Point the camera at the QR codeHold steady 15–30 cm away.
  5. 5
    OR type the ID in "qrManualInput"If camera fails.
  6. 6
    System auto-fillsqrMemberId, qrCheckInTime, qrCheckInDate, qrCheckInLocation.
  7. 7
    Verify the officer's name
  8. 8
    Click "Confirm Check-In"✓ Confirm
  9. 9
    Confirm the success message
  10. 10
    Scan the next officer

Method C — Bulk Upload

  1. 1
    Prepare a CSV fileColumns: AttDate, AttTime, AttName, AttId, Status, Notes.
  2. 2
    Example CSV:
    AttDate,AttTime,AttName,AttId,Status,Notes
    15/05/2026,08:15,Abebe Kebede,12345,Present,
    15/05/2026,08:45,Sara Mekonnen,12346,Late,Traffic
  3. 3
    Click "Attendance Insertion" → "Bulk Upload"
  4. 4
    Click "Choose File"📎 Choose File
  5. 5
    Upload your CSV
  6. 6
    Click "Upload & Validate"
  7. 7
    Review the preview
  8. 8
    Fix and re-uploadIf any rows are red.
  9. 9
    Click "Confirm & Save All"✓ Save All
  10. 10
    Read the summary
✅ What happens next
  • Attendance records are saved
  • They appear in "Attendance Data"

➕14. Register a Peace Keeper

13
Register a New Peace Keeper
Add a new officer to your ketena
What it does Registers a new officer in the system. They get a unique system ID and appear in the officer list.
Before you start
  • Officer's full name
  • Identity number (government ID)
  • Phone number (10 digits, starts with 09)
  • Email (optional)
  • Area (Sub City + Ketena) — auto-filled to yours
  • Address
  • Clear portrait photo (optional)
Left menu → Register Peace Keeper
  1. 1
    Click "Register Peace Keeper"The registration form opens.
  2. 2
    Type the full name in "pkFullName"Complete legal name.
  3. 3
    Type the identity number in "pkIdentityNo"Government-issued ID.
  4. 4
    Type the phone in "pkPhone"Format: 0912345678
  5. 5
    Type the email in "pkEmail" (optional)
  6. 6
    "pkArea" is auto-filledTo your Sub City.
  7. 7
    "pkKetena" is auto-filledTo your ketena.
  8. 8
    Type "pkAddress" (optional)Full home address.
  9. 9
    Upload a photo (optional)Click 📎 Choose File — JPG/PNG, max 5 MB.
  10. 10
    Review all fields carefullyID and phone must be unique.
  11. 11
    Click "Register" or "Save"✓ Register
  12. 12
    Confirm the green success message
  13. 13
    Note the new officer's system ID
✅ What happens next
  • The new officer appears in "View Peace Keepers"
  • They can be assigned to schedules by higher roles
🔒
Your ketena only Officers you register are assigned to your ketena. You cannot register officers for other ketenas.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Duplicate identity number
  • Wrong phone format
  • Missing full name
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Identity No already exists" → Check for duplicate
  • "Phone number invalid" → 10 digits, starts with 09
  • "File too large" → Photo under 5 MB

📁15. Register a Crime Case

14
Register a New Crime Case
File a formal incident report
What it does Records a new crime case in the system for your ketena.
Before you start
  • Case title (short description)
  • Crime type (Theft / Assault / Fraud / Violence / Drug / Other)
  • Location details
  • Date of the incident
  • Full narrative of what happened
  • Any attachments (photos, documents)
Left menu → Register Crime
  1. 1
    Click "Register Crime"The crime form opens.
  2. 2
    Type the case title in "crimeTitle"Short, descriptive name.
  3. 3
    Choose "crimeType"From the dropdown: Theft / Assault / Fraud / Violence / Drug / Other.
  4. 4
    Type "crimeLocation"Exact location or landmark.
  5. 5
    Set "crimeDate"Date of the incident.
  6. 6
    Type "crimeDescription"Full narrative — who, what, when, where, how.
  7. 7
    Type "crimeReporter" (optional)Name of the person who reported it.
  8. 8
    Attach evidence (optional)Click 📎 Choose File — photos, PDFs, up to 10 MB each.
  9. 9
    Review all fieldsMake sure date and location are correct.
  10. 10
    Click "Register Crime Case"✓ Register
  11. 11
    Confirm the success message
  12. 12
    Note the assigned case numberFor future reference.
✅ What happens next
  • The case appears in "View Crime Cases"
  • District Admin and above can view it
🔒
Your ketena only Cases you register are tagged with your ketena. They appear in your list.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Wrong date format — use dd/mm/yyyy
  • Too short a description — write the full story
  • Not attaching available evidence
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Required field missing" → Check red-highlighted fields
  • "Invalid date" → Use dd/mm/yyyy format
  • "File too large" → Under 10 MB per file
