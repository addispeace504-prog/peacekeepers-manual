🌆1. What You Can Do as City Admin

As City Admin, you have one of the two highest access levels in the system. You see the entire city across all sub-cities and can act on city-wide data.

ℹ️
Your scope You see all sub-cities at once. When you analyze crime or view peace keepers, the data covers the entire Addis Ababa city area — not just one sub-city.

Your 18 Permissions

# Permission What it does
1 Log In Access the system
2 Dashboard See the city-wide overview
3 Update Account Edit your own profile
4 Change PIN Update your login PIN
5 View Peace Keepers See all officers in the city
6 View Crime Cases See all cases in the city
7 Analyze Peace Keepers Deployment analytics
8 Analyze Crimes Crime pattern analytics
9 Crime Geo Location View crimes on a map
10 Send SMS Send city-wide messages
11 Office Communication Internal messages
12 Active Discussions Join live discussions
13 Call History See past discussions
14 Request Certificate Request a certificate
15 Manage Certificates Approve/reject certificate requests
16 My Certificates See your own certificate requests
17 Developer Support Contact support
18 Log Out End your session
⚠️
What you cannot do (unlike Super Admin) You cannot manage users, restore backups, or change system settings. Those are Super Admin-only permissions.

🔐2. Log In to the System

1
Log In
Required for every session
What it does Opens the system with your City Admin identity so you can access city-wide data and tools.
Before you start
  • Your username (given by the Super Admin)
  • Your PIN (6+ digits)
  • A modern browser and internet connection
https://www.aapskeeper.gov.et
  1. 1
    Open your browserLaunch Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  2. 2
    Go to the login pageType www.aapskeeper.gov.et and press Enter.
  3. 3
    Click the Username fieldA cursor appears.
  4. 4
    Type your usernameExactly as given by the Super Admin.
  5. 5
    Click the PIN field
  6. 6
    Type your PINEach digit shows as a dot.
  7. 7
    Click Login➜ Login
  8. 8
    Wait for the dashboardLoads in 2–5 seconds.
✅ What happens next
  • You land on the Dashboard
  • The left menu shows City Admin options
  • Your name appears top-right
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Invalid username or PIN" → Check spelling and caps
  • "Account locked" → Wait 15 minutes
  • Page won't load → Refresh (F5), try another browser

💬11. Send SMS

10
Send an SMS to Peace Keepers
City-wide mass messaging
What it does Sends an SMS to selected peace keepers or groups. As City Admin, you can send to the entire city or a specific sub-city.
Before you start
  • The message text (max 160 characters per SMS)
  • Recipients — all officers, one sub-city, or individuals
  • A short subject line
Left menu → Send SMS
  1. 1
    Click "Send SMS"The SMS composer opens.
  2. 2
    Choose recipientsAll Peace Keepers / Filter by Sub City / Individual search.
  3. 3
    Tick the checkboxesOr click "Select All".
  4. 4
    Type a subjectShort title, e.g. "Monday meeting".
  5. 5
    Type the messageUnder 160 characters.
  6. 6
    Choose SMS typeAlert / Schedule / Announcement / Emergency.
  7. 7
    Choose sender typeDefault or Name-Based.
  8. 8
    Set end date (if scheduled)Format dd/mm/yyyy.
  9. 9
    Attach a file (optional)📎 Attach
  10. 10
    Click "Send to Approval"📤 Send to Approval
  11. 11
    Confirm it's in the approval queueYou'll see it listed with a "Pending" status.
✅ What happens next
  • The message appears in the approval queue
  • A supervisor approves it
  • Once approved, it sends automatically
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Sending to "All" when you meant one sub-city
  • Message over 160 characters (becomes multiple SMS)
  • No subject — hard to find later
💡
Can't send directly? As City Admin, you can approve SMS as well — but the standard flow is to send to approval first so a supervisor can double-check before it goes out.
