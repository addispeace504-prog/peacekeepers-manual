🎯1. Welcome & What This Manual Covers

Welcome to the Super Admin Guide — the most complete manual in the Peace Keepers series. As Super Admin, you have access to every part of the system. This guide shows you exactly how to use each permission, step by step.

ℹ️
Who this guide is for Only users whose account type is set to Super Admin. If your account type is something else (City, District, Staff, etc.), you should read the guide that matches your role.

What You'll Learn

  • How to log in and navigate the dashboard
  • How to update your own account and PIN
  • How to communicate with other users (Office Communication, SMS)
  • How to create, edit, and delete users of every type
  • How to review activity logs for accountability
  • How to manage system data
  • How to create and restore database backups
  • How to configure system-wide settings
  • How to contact developer support
💡
How to use this guide Every permission is presented as a card. Read the "What it does" first, then follow the steps. If something goes wrong, look for the red "If something goes wrong" section at the bottom of the card.

👥2. The 9 User Types (Overview)

The Peace Keepers system supports 9 different user types. Each one has a specific set of permissions based on their role and location in the administrative hierarchy.

# User Type Scope Summary
1 Super Admin City-wide + system Full access to everything. Manages users, backups, and system settings.
2 City Admin City-wide Full access to data. Manages SMS, certificates, and sees all sub-cities.
3 Sub City System Admin One sub-city Technical admin of a sub-city. Manages users within their sub-city.
4 Sub City Admin One sub-city Executive admin of a sub-city. Approves certificates, sends SMS.
5 Sub City One sub-city Sub-city user. Views data and submits community reports.
6 District System Admin One district Technical admin of a district. Manages users and identity cards.
7 District Admin One district District executive. Manages schedules, attendance, and officers.
8 Staff Assigned area Day-to-day data entry. Registers officers and crimes.
9 Ketena One ketena Neighborhood-level user. Registers officers and crimes for their ketena.
⚠️
You are Super Admin Your account has the highest level of access. Every permission in the other 8 guides is also available to you. This guide documents your exclusive permissions plus the shared ones.

🔐3. How to Log In

1
Log In to the System
Required for every session
What it does Opens the system with your identity so you can access your role's features. Without logging in, no page works.
Before you start
  • Your username (given by the administrator who created your account)
  • Your PIN (6+ digit number)
  • A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari)
  • Internet connection
https://www.aapskeeper.gov.et
  1. 1
    Open your browser Launch Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari on your computer or phone.
  2. 2
    Go to the login page Type www.aapskeeper.gov.et in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. 3
    Wait for the page to load You'll see the Addis Ababa city seal, green background, and the login form.
  4. 4
    Click the Username field A cursor appears inside the box.
  5. 5
    Type your username For example: SUPER_AD (exactly as your administrator gave it)
  6. 6
    Click the PIN field A cursor appears inside the box.
  7. 7
    Type your PIN Each digit shows as a dot • • • • • •
  8. 8
    Click the Login button ➜ Login
  9. 9
    Wait for the dashboard After 2–5 seconds, the dashboard opens.
✅ What happens next
  • You're on the Dashboard page
  • Your name appears in the top-right corner
  • The left menu shows Super Admin options
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Typing the PIN with wrong digits — dots are your only feedback
  • Adding spaces before or after the username
  • Using Caps Lock when it isn't needed
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Invalid username or PIN" → Check spelling and caps. Try again.
  • "Account locked" → Wait 15 minutes before retrying.
  • Page won't load → Check internet. Refresh with F5. Try another browser.

🔑6. Change Your PIN

4
Change Your PIN
Recommended every 3 months
What it does Updates your login PIN. You must know your current PIN to set a new one.
Left menu → Update Account → Change PIN section
  1. 1
    Open Update Account Click "Update Account" in the left menu.
  2. 2
    Scroll to the Change PIN section Look for three fields labeled Previous PIN, New PIN, Confirm PIN.
  3. 3
    Type your current PIN in "Previous PIN" This proves the change is really you.
  4. 4
    Type your new PIN in "New PIN" Use at least 6 digits, no 123456 or 111111.
  5. 5
    Retype it in "Confirm PIN" Both must match exactly.
  6. 6
    Click Save ✓ Save
  7. 7
    Log out and log back in with the new PIN This confirms the change worked.
✅ What happens next
  • Your old PIN stops working immediately
  • Your new PIN is required for every future login
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Forgetting the new PIN right after changing
  • Reusing a PIN from another system
  • Choosing a PIN based on birth year or phone number
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Previous PIN incorrect" → Try again, carefully
  • "PIN too short" → Use at least 6 digits
  • Forgot your current PIN → Use "Forgot PIN" on the login page

➕10. Create a New User

9
Create a New User
Add any of the 9 user types
What it does Adds a brand-new user account to the system. You can create users of any role: City, Sub City System Admin, Sub City Admin, Sub City, District System Admin, District, Staff, Ketena, or another Super Admin.
Before you start
  • The user's full name
  • Their phone number (10 digits, starts with 09)
  • Their email (optional but recommended)
  • The role they should have
  • A temporary PIN (6+ digits)
  • Their assigned area (Sub City, District)
Left menu → User Management → "Create New User" button
  1. 1
    Click "User Management" The user list opens.
  2. 2
    Click "Create New User" ➕ Create New User A modal form opens.
  3. 3
    Type the username Unique, lowercase, no spaces. E.g. abebe.k
  4. 4
    Type the full name Complete legal name.
  5. 5
    Choose the User Type Dropdown with 9 options:
    • Super Admin
    • City Admin
    • Sub City System Admin
    • Sub City Admin
    • Sub City
    • District System Admin
    • District Admin
    • Staff
    • Ketena
  6. 6
    Type and confirm the PIN At least 6 digits, same value in both boxes.
  7. 7
    Type the phone number Format: 0912345678
  8. 8
    Type the email (optional) E.g. abebe@aaps.gov.et
  9. 9
    Choose the Sub City If the user is assigned to a sub-city.
  10. 10
    Choose the District If the user is assigned to a district.
  11. 11
    Click "Create User" ✓ Create User
  12. 12
    Confirm the green success message "User created successfully"
✅ What happens next
  • The new user appears in the User Management list
  • They can log in immediately with the PIN you set
  • They should change their PIN on first login
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Duplicate username — the system will reject it
  • Wrong User Type — hard to fix later
  • Weak PIN — must be at least 6 digits
  • Skipping Sub City for sub-city users
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Username already exists" → pick a different username
  • "PIN too short" → use 6+ digits
  • "Phone number invalid" → must be 10 digits, starts with 09
  • "Email invalid" → must contain @ and a domain

💾16. Create a Backup

15
Create a New Backup
Snapshot of everything, right now
What it does Takes a complete snapshot of all data in the system — every table, every record, every file reference. Can be restored later if anything goes wrong.
Best practice
  • Create a backup every Sunday night
  • Always create one before major changes
  • Download a copy to external storage monthly
Left menu → Database Backups → "Create New Backup"
  1. 1
    Click "Database Backups" The backup list opens.
  2. 2
    Click "Create New Backup" 💾 Create New Backup
  3. 3
    Wait for the progress bar Small databases take 1–2 minutes. Large ones may take up to 10 minutes.
  4. 4
    Wait for the completion message "Backup completed successfully"
  5. 5
    Find the new backup in the list It shows today's date and time.
  6. 6
    Optional: Download for offsite storage Click ⬇️ Download to save a copy to your computer.
✅ What happens next
  • A new backup file appears in the list
  • The file is stored in \backups\ on the server
  • You can restore from it any time
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Assuming the backup is offsite — it's still on the server
  • Deleting old backups before confirming new ones work
  • Forgetting that backups take time for large databases

↺17. Restore from Backup

16
Restore the Database from a Backup
Replaces all current data
🚨
This is the most dangerous action in the system Restoring overwrites everything. Any data entered after the backup date will be permanently lost. Always notify your team before restoring. Only do this when it's truly necessary.
Before you start
  • Notify all users — they will lose access during the restore
  • Have the backup date ready
  • Create a fresh backup first (so nothing is lost)
  • Do it during off-hours (evening or weekend)
Left menu → Database Backups → click backup → Restore
  1. 1
    Open "Database Backups" See the list of available backups.
  2. 2
    Find the backup you want to restore Look for the date you need.
  3. 3
    Click "Restore" on that row ↺ Restore
  4. 4
    Read the warning carefully The system will ask you to confirm.
  5. 5
    Type CONFIRM in the confirmation box Or "RESTORE" — whichever the system asks for.
  6. 6
    Click "Yes, Restore Now" The restore begins.
  7. 7
    Wait for completion This can take 5–15 minutes.
  8. 8
    Log out and log back in The system will force a fresh session.
  9. 9
    Verify the data matches the backup date Check a few recent records.
  10. 10
    Notify all users that the system is back online They may need to re-login.
✅ What happens next
  • All current data is replaced by the backup data
  • Any records created after the backup date are gone
  • The system resumes from the backup state
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Restoring during business hours — disrupts everyone
  • Not telling users — they'll be confused
  • Restoring without a current backup — data loss risk
  • Picking the wrong backup date
