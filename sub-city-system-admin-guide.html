⚙️1. What You Can Do as Sub City System Admin

As Sub City System Admin, you are the technical administrator for one sub-city. You manage the users, data quality, and bulk operations within your sub-city — but you do not see or affect other sub-cities.

🔒
Your scope: one sub-city Your permissions are limited to the sub-city you're assigned to. You cannot view, edit, or manage data belonging to other sub-cities. When you create users, they must belong to your sub-city.

Your 11 Permissions

# Permission What it does
1 Log In Access the system
2 Dashboard See your sub-city overview
3 Update Account Edit your own profile
4 Change PIN Update your login PIN
5 System Overview Sub-city technical stats
6 Office Communication Internal messages
7 View SMS See SMS history
8 User Management Create/edit/delete users in your sub-city
9 Encoding Converter Fix scrambled Amharic text
10 Bulk Registration Register many officers at once
11 Developer Support Contact support
12 Log Out End your session
⚠️
What you cannot do You cannot modify security-category settings (only Super Admin can). You also cannot restore backups, manage system settings above your sub-city, or approve certificates.

  6. 6

➕10. Create a New User

10
Create a New User in Your Sub-City
Add Sub City Admin, Staff, Ketena, or District roles
What it does Adds a new user account assigned to your sub-city. The user will have access only within your sub-city.
Roles you can create
  • Sub City Admin
  • Sub City Staff
  • District System Admin
  • District Admin
  • Staff
  • Ketena
  • Block (if applicable)
Before you start
  • Their full name
  • Their phone number (10 digits, starts with 09)
  • Their email (optional)
  • The role they should have
  • A temporary PIN
Left menu → User Management → "Create New User"
  1. 1
    Open User Management
  2. 2
    Click "Create New User"➕ Create New User
  3. 3
    Type the usernameUnique, lowercase, no spaces. E.g. sara.m
  4. 4
    Type the full name
  5. 5
    Choose the User TypeOptions: Sub City Admin, Sub City Staff, District System Admin, District Admin, Staff, Ketena.
  6. 6
    Type and confirm the PINAt least 6 digits.
  7. 7
    Type the phone numberFormat: 0912345678
  8. 8
    Type the email (optional)
  9. 9
    Sub City is auto-set to your sub-cityYou cannot change this.
  10. 10
    Choose the DistrictIf applicable.
  11. 11
    Click "Create User"✓ Create User
  12. 12
    Confirm the success message"User created successfully"
✅ What happens next
  • The new user appears in the User Management list
  • They can log in immediately with the PIN you set
  • They should change PIN on first login
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Duplicate username — the system will reject it
  • Wrong User Type — hard to fix later
  • Weak PIN — at least 6 digits
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Username already exists" → Pick a different username
  • "PIN too short" → Use 6+ digits
  • "Phone number invalid" → 10 digits, starts with 09

📤13. Bulk Registration

13
Register Many Peace Keepers at Once
Upload a file instead of typing each one
What it does Lets you register many peace keepers in one go by uploading a CSV or Excel file. Perfect for onboarding a batch of new recruits.
Before you start
  • A CSV or Excel file with one row per officer
  • Columns: Full Name, ID, Phone, Area, Ketena
  • File size under 10 MB
  • No blank rows
Left menu → Bulk Registration
  1. 1
    Prepare your CSV fileOpen Excel or a text editor. Create columns matching the required fields.
  2. 2
    Example CSV structure:
    FullName,IdentityNo,Phone,Area,Ketena
    Abebe Kebede,1234567890,0912345678,Bole,Ketena 04
    Sara Mekonnen,1234567891,0912345679,Bole,Ketena 04
  3. 3
    Start with 3 rows firstTest that the format works before uploading the full file.
  4. 4
    Click "Bulk Registration"in the left menu.
  5. 5
    Click "Choose File"📎 Choose File
  6. 6
    Select your CSV or Excel filefrom your computer.
  7. 7
    Click "Upload & Validate"📤 Upload & Validate
  8. 8
    Wait for validationThe system checks each row.
  9. 9
    Review the preview tableValid rows appear green, invalid rows appear red with the reason.
  10. 10
    Fix any errors and re-uploadIf rows are red, fix in Excel and upload again.
  11. 11
    Click "Confirm & Save All"✓ Confirm & Save All
  12. 12
    Read the final summary"Saved: 47 — Failed: 3" with details.
✅ What happens next
  • All valid officers are registered
  • They appear in the View Peace Keepers list
  • Failed rows are logged with reasons
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Column headers not matching exactly
  • Blank rows in the file
  • Date format wrong (should be dd/mm/yyyy)
  • Duplicates — same ID twice in the file
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Column headers not found" → Check spelling, must match exactly
  • "Duplicate ID" → Remove the duplicate row
  • "File too large" → Split into smaller files under 10 MB
  • "Invalid phone number" → Must be 10 digits
🎯
Sub-city restricted Every officer you register via bulk upload is assigned to your sub-city. You cannot bulk-register officers for other sub-cities.
