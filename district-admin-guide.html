🏛️1. What You Can Do as District Admin

As District Admin, you are the executive administrator for one district (woreda). You run operations — sending SMS, managing attendance, creating schedules, overseeing peace keepers, and requesting certificates.

🔒
Your scope: one district Your permissions are limited to the district you're assigned to. You cannot view or manage data from other districts or sub-cities.

🔐2. Log In to the System

1
Log In
Required for every session
What it does Opens the system with your District Admin identity so you can access district tools.
Before you start
  • Your username (given by the administrator who created your account)
  • Your PIN (6+ digits)
  • A modern browser and internet connection
https://www.aapskeeper.gov.et
  1. 1
    Open your browserLaunch Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
  2. 2
    Go to the login pageType www.aapskeeper.gov.et and press Enter.
  3. 3
    Wait for the page to loadYou'll see the Addis Ababa city seal and login form.
  4. 4
    Click the Username field
  5. 5
    Type your usernameExactly as given.
  6. 6
    Click the PIN field
  7. 7
    Type your PINEach digit shows as a dot.
  8. 8
    Click Login➜ Login
  9. 9
    Wait for the dashboardLoads in 2–5 seconds.
✅ What happens next
  • You land on the Dashboard
  • Your location label shows "District [your region]"
  • The left menu shows District Admin options
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Invalid username or PIN" → Check spelling and caps
  • "Account locked" → Wait 15 minutes
  • Page won't load → Refresh (F5), try another browser
  • Forgot PIN → Use "Forgot PIN" on the login page

📅20. Attendance Insertion

19
Record Peace Keeper Attendance
Mark who is present, late, or absent
What it does Records attendance for peace keepers in your district. You can record individuals, check in by QR, or upload a bulk file.
Three attendance methods
  • Individual — type the officer's name and mark status
  • QR Check-In — scan the officer's ID card
  • Bulk Upload — upload a CSV/Excel file for many officers at once
Left menu → Attendance Insertion

Method A — Individual Check-In

  1. 1
    Click "Attendance Insertion"The attendance form opens.
  2. 2
    Type the officer's name in "attName"A dropdown suggests matches as you type.
  3. 3
    Click the officer from the dropdownThe officer's ID is auto-filled.
  4. 4
    Set "attDate"Use the calendar picker (Ethiopian calendar dd/mm/yyyy).
  5. 5
    Set "attTime"24-hour format (e.g. 08:30).
  6. 6
    Choose "attStatus"Present / Late / Absent / On Leave.
  7. 7
    Type "attNotes" (optional)Reason for late/absent.
  8. 8
    Click "Save Attendance"✓ Save
  9. 9
    Confirm the success messageGreen: "Attendance recorded successfully".
  10. 10
    Repeat for the next officerThe form clears automatically.

Method B — QR Check-In

  1. 1
    Click "Attendance Insertion"
  2. 2
    Choose "QR Check-In"tab or mode.
  3. 3
    Allow camera accessIf prompted, click "Allow".
  4. 4
    Point the camera at the officer's QR codeHold steady 15–30 cm away.
  5. 5
    OR type the ID in "qrManualInput"If camera fails.
  6. 6
    The system auto-fillsqrMemberId, qrCheckInTime, qrCheckInDate, qrCheckInLocation.
  7. 7
    Verify the officer's name
  8. 8
    Click "Confirm Check-In"✓ Confirm
  9. 9
    Confirm the success message
  10. 10
    Scan the next officer

Method C — Bulk Upload

  1. 1
    Prepare a CSV fileColumns: AttDate, AttTime, AttName, AttId, Status, Notes.
  2. 2
    Example CSV:
    AttDate,AttTime,AttName,AttId,Status,Notes
    15/05/2026,08:15,Abebe Kebede,12345,Present,
    15/05/2026,08:45,Sara Mekonnen,12346,Late,Traffic
  3. 3
    Click "Attendance Insertion" → "Bulk Upload"
  4. 4
    Click "Choose File"📎 Choose File
  5. 5
    Upload your CSV
  6. 6
    Click "Upload & Validate"
  7. 7
    Review the previewGreen rows = valid, red = errors.
  8. 8
    Fix and re-uploadIf any rows are red.
  9. 9
    Click "Confirm & Save All"✓ Save All
  10. 10
    Read the summary"Saved: 47 — Failed: 3".
✅ What happens next
  • Attendance records are saved
  • They appear in "Attendance Data"
  • Officers' attendance history updates
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Wrong date format (must be dd/mm/yyyy)
  • Skipping attStatus (required)
  • CSV column headers don't match exactly

  9. 9

  10. 10
    Click "Create Schedule"✓ Create Schedule
  11. 11
    Confirm the success messageOfficers will receive SMS if selected.

  • The schedule is saved
  • Officers can see it on their dashboard
  • SMS notifications are sent if selected
💡
View existing schedules Use the "filterDay" and "filterShift" dropdowns to view schedules already created.
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Assigning the same officer to two overlapping shifts
  • Forgetting to notify officers by SMS
  • Choosing wrong day/shift combination
