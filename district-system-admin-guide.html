⚙️1. What You Can Do as District System Admin

As District System Admin, you are the technical administrator for one district (woreda). You manage users, configure the geographic structure, and issue identity cards.

🔒
Your scope: one district Your permissions are limited to the district you're assigned to. You cannot view or manage data from other districts or sub-cities.

Your 11 Permissions

# Permission What it does
1Log InAccess the system
2DashboardDistrict overview
3Update AccountEdit your own profile
4Change PINUpdate your login PIN
5Office CommunicationInternal messaging
6View SMSSee SMS history
7Manage Identity Cards⭐ Unique to this role — request, approve, print
8User ManagementCreate/edit users in your district
9Configure AreaManage area hierarchy
10Developer SupportContact support
11Log OutEnd your session
⚠️
What you cannot do You cannot modify security-category settings (only Super Admin can). You also cannot restore backups, manage SMS, or approve certificates.

🪪9. Manage Identity Cards — Overview

What this section does This is the only role in the entire system that can manage Identity Cards. You can:
  • Request a new identity card
  • Approve or reject identity card requests
  • Print approved identity cards
  • Revoke an identity card if necessary
⭐
Unique permission Only District System Admin has this permission. If you have any questions, contact your Super Admin or City Admin.

What is an Identity Card?

An Identity Card is an official document issued to a peace keeper, staff member, or community member. It contains their photo, name, unique card number, and a QR code for verification. Cards are printed and can be verified in the field using the QR code.

📝10. Request an Identity Card

8
Submit an Identity Card Request
For a peace keeper, staff member, or citizen
What it does Submits a request for a new identity card. The request goes to the approval queue and, once approved, the card can be printed.
Before you start
  • Applicant's full name
  • Applicant's role (Peace Keeper / Staff / Citizen)
  • Applicant's Sub City and District
  • Applicant's Ketena and Blok
  • Event name (if applicable)
  • Event year and date
  • Reason for the request
  • Clear portrait photo (JPG/PNG, max 5 MB)
Left menu → Manage Identity Cards → "New Request"
  1. 1
    Click "Manage Identity Cards"The Identity Card page opens.
  2. 2
    Click "New Identity Card Request"➕ New Request
  3. 3
    Type "Recipient Name"Applicant's full legal name.
  4. 4
    Type "Recipient Role"For example "Peace Keeper", "Staff", "Citizen".
  5. 5
    Select "Recipient Sub City"From the dropdown.
  6. 6
    Select "Recipient District"From the dropdown.
  7. 7
    Type "Recipient Ketena"Neighborhood (optional).
  8. 8
    Type "Recipient Blok"Block (optional).
  9. 9
    Type "Event Name"If this is for a special event, enter its name.
  10. 10
    Type "Event Year"Ethiopian calendar year.
  11. 11
    Set "Event Date"Use the date picker.
  12. 12
    Type "Reason"Why the card is needed (required).
  13. 13
    Upload applicant's photoClick 📎 Choose File — JPG/PNG, max 5 MB, clear face.
  14. 14
    Review all fieldsMake sure everything is correct.
  15. 15
    Click "Submit Request"✓ Submit Request
  16. 16
    Note your tracking numberUse it to follow the request.
✅ What happens next
  • Request appears in the "Pending" list
  • A supervisor or admin reviews it
  • Once approved, the card is generated as PDF
⚠️ Common mistakes to avoid
  • Missing photo — cannot print without it
  • Photo blurry or too dark
  • Wrong Sub City / District
  • Missing reason field
🚨 If something goes wrong
  • "Photo required" → Upload a JPG or PNG image
  • "File too large" → Compress under 5 MB
  • "Recipient name required" → Fill the required field
